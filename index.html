AI LAB

Reinforcement learning is part of my graduation project (PPO in MuJoCo). This is the small, readable version: tabular Q-learning in plain JavaScript, running in your browser. The robot S learns to reach the goal G. Click any cell to move a shelf (#) and watch it relearn the route.

episodes: 0

Arrows show the best action the robot has learned for each cell. Brighter means higher value. Rewards: +1 at the goal, a small penalty per step, a larger one for bumping into a shelf.

Not PPO, but the same loop: act, get a reward, update, repeat. For deep learning see Kemet, the land-type classifier.
